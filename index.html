Fourier Analysis of Gravitational Waves

A browser based simulation that describes the Fourier analysis of a gravitational wave signal. The signal is generated to mimic the behavior of a real gravitational wave, and the Fourier transform is used to analyse its frequency components.

Mathematical Idea

Why Fourier analysis is useful

A gravitational wave signal changes over time, so the time-domain graph shows how strain varies as the wave passes through a detector. The Fourier transform converts this time-based signal into the frequency domain, allowing us to identify which frequencies dominate the signal.

In real gravitational wave detection, this is important because compact binary mergers creates chirp signals. The frequency and amplitude increase as the objects spiral closer together.
